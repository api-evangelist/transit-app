Transit app has partnered with TransLink as the recommended trip-planning application for Metro Vancouver, offering all riders access to Transit Royale features. The partnership includes enhanced ETAs, schedules, real-time detour detection, and rider feedback integration, with special preparations for the FIFA World Cup 2026 tournament.
